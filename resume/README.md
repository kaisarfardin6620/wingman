This folder contains everything you need to build and customise your professional resume.

## How to Use

### Step 1 — Review the Draft
Open `RESUME_DRAFT.md`. This is your complete resume draft with all projects and skills extracted from your actual GitHub code.

### Step 2 — Fill in Missing Details
Search for `_... — To be added_` placeholders and fill in:
- University name(s) and graduation year(s)
- Any internship or work experience
- Location / phone number

### Step 3 — Tailor for Each Job
Before applying to a specific role:
1. Read the job description carefully.
2. Copy the 3–4 projects most relevant to that role from `projects_bullet_bank.md`.
3. Match keyword language in your Summary to the job posting.

### Step 4 — Export to PDF
- Paste your finished markdown into a Google Doc or Word
- Use a clean single-column layout
- Export as PDF (ATS systems prefer single-column PDFs)

---

## Tips for ATS Optimisation

- **Use exact keywords from the job description** in your skills and summary sections.
- **Quantify results** wherever possible: "Reduced API latency by 40%", "Handled 500+ concurrent WebSocket sessions".
- **One page** for less than 3 years of experience; **two pages** acceptable for 3+ years.
- **Avoid tables, images, and multi-column layouts** in the version you submit to ATS systems.
- **Strong action verbs:** Architected, Engineered, Built, Deployed, Optimised, Designed, Integrated, Reduced, Improved.
